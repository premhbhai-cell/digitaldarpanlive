हमारे बारे में About Us

Digital Darpan Live is a news platform from Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh. Our promise is simple: सच, निष्पक्ष और बेबाक खबरों का भरोसेमंद नाम – true news and a fearless voice.

We bring you reports from villages, blocks, districts and the state capital – on local issues, politics, crime, education, farming, sports and culture – through our website, YouTube channel, Facebook page and X account.

What we stand for

  • Facts first, checked before they are published.
  • A voice for people in rural and small-town Madhya Pradesh.
  • Respect for readers, sources and the law.
